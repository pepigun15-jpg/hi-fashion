How nearest-stock matching will work

For each product, the live platform will check that stock is available, that the seller ships to the buyer's destination, and where the item can dispatch from. It will then rank eligible offers by distance to the dispatch location and show estimated delivery and shipping cost where supplied. We will not claim an item is nearby unless the seller confirms its stock location and availability.
